# hold drill — design

The second drill, and the one that matters more. The first proved a signature can be
given from a phone. This one asks what happens when the answer is **no**.

## Please tap Hold, or type `no #N`

Approving would tell us nothing new. The refusal path has never run.

## What should happen

The reply resolves to the chief seat exactly as an approval does — the account is an
identity, and what it may do is the post's business. Then nothing is signed, and the
task stays where it was rather than advancing.

## What is genuinely unknown

Whether a refusal leaves a record at all.

An approval writes a ledger line, because a signature is a thing that happened. A
refusal is the absence of one — and an absence looks identical to never having been
asked. If holding leaves no trace, then "I said no" and "nobody replied" are the same
state, which is a worse gap than the missing receipt on the approval side.

That is the question this drill answers, and either answer is worth having.
